# Browser Game Systems Comparison

Sources checked May 24, 2026:
- The West official page: https://www.innogames.com/en/games/the-west
- The West jobs support: https://support.innogames.com/kb/TheWest/en_DK/4707/Jobs
- Ikariam official page: https://gameforge.com/en-GB/games/ikariam.html
- OGame publisher page: https://gameforge.itch.io/ogame

## What We Already Have
- Timed map travel.
- Job queue with delayed rewards.
- Equipment, inventory upgrades, and rare gems.
- Player/minion roster progression.
- Town shops and rotating city quests.
- Combat reports through activity history.
- Temporary dungeons and world enemies.

## The West Lessons
The West leans hard on jobs, danger, duels, equipment tools, towns, and shared fort battles.

Useful additions for our game:
- Job danger rating: safer jobs pay less, cursed jobs can injure the commander or trigger combat.
- Job tool bonuses: certain equipment can improve specific job categories.
- Town project board: players donate gold/gems/job results to unlock town perks.
- Duel/battle reports: give every fight a readable report card with damage, losses, and loot.

## Ikariam Lessons
Ikariam's strongest long-term loop is city growth: resources, research, buildings, trade, diplomacy, and alliances.

Useful additions for our game:
- Town reputation: Harborview and Riverside reputation unlocks better contracts and shop stock.
- Research/lore library: long timers that unlock passive bonuses, spell upgrades, and unit tactics.
- Resource-lite economy: keep gold/gems, but add two thematic town resources later, such as Relics and Provisions.
- Alliance orders: player groups can share town perks and dungeon objectives.

## OGame Lessons
OGame is built around timers, scouting, fleet preservation, resources, research, alliances, and risk while offline.

Useful additions for our game:
- Scout reports before combat: reveal enemy minions and estimated power.
- March reports: every long travel, dungeon, and PVP challenge becomes a log entry.
- Army preservation rules: PVP should risk gold and pride, not permanent unit deletion.
- Scheduled threats: world bosses or dungeon breaches announce a future time so players plan around it.

## Recommended Next Systems
1. Add town reputation for Harborview/Riverside.
2. Add scout reports for enemies and dungeons.
3. Add job danger ratings and tool bonuses.
4. Add a research/lore library with long timers.
5. Add town projects after reputation exists.
6. Add alliances only after player-to-player value is clearer.

## Keep Out For Now
- Full city building.
- Many resource types.
- Offline raiding.
- Permanent PVP army loss.
- Deep alliance war rules.

Those systems can make the game feel more like admin work than a dark fantasy RPG. The better path is one strong timer loop at a time: travel, jobs, scouting, research, then town projects.
